## Executive Summary

Walterin is the artistic brand of Slovak illustrator **Walter Ihring**. It is built on two equal pillars — **illustration and text** — and produces physical objects that turn Walter's authored universe into things people keep: decks, books, prints. The first product is **Comics Tarot of Consciousness**, a 78-card illustrated tarot deck.

The market opportunity is clear. Tarot is a saturated category dominated by two ends — mystical mass-market decks (interchangeable, hollow) and art-house decks (often beautiful but story-thin). Walterin sits in a third lane: **a deck that reads like a graphic novel you can shuffle**. The work is illustrated by a single hand, set in a single universe, and authored natively in two languages. That combination is the moat.

The positioning thesis: **An illustrated story you can keep.**

## Category Landscape

### How tarot decks are usually positioned
| Position | Typical brand cues | Buyer expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Mystical / mass-market | Generic symbolism, gold foil, esoteric copy, "spiritual tools" framing | Divinatory utility |
| Art-house / artist deck | Stylized illustration, limited runs, art-fair aesthetic | Decorative object + niche use |
| Pop-culture licensed | Brand IP (Marvel, Disney, etc.), collector framing | Fandom collectible |
| Therapy-adjacent | Jungian / shadow-work / mindfulness framing | Self-help tool |

### Where Walterin sits
**Walterin = an illustrated comic story in deck form.** Each card is a panel from a coherent narrative universe drawn by one person. The deck rewards readers and lookers, not just users. It is a collectible-illustrated-object first, a usable tarot deck second.

This frame is the line we hold across every channel.

## Differentiation

### Where Walterin wins

| Dimension | Mass-market decks | Art-house decks | Walterin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authorship | Anonymous / publisher-led | Single artist, often one project | Single artist, ongoing universe |
| Story coherence | Stock symbol set | Often standalone aesthetic | Narrative universe across products |
| Material identity | Standardized stock | Variable | Considered packaging, brand-owned tuck-box anatomy |
| Voice | Generic spiritual | Often silent on copy | Bold, warm, literary; SK + EN native |
| Origin story | None | Often vague | Slovak illustrator with existing readership |
| Future products | None planned | Rarely a series | Planned product universe extending the same world |

### Core differentiators (ranked by uniqueness)

1. **Story-first illustration.** Walter is a comic illustrator first. The cards are scenes from a universe, not symbol charts dressed in art.
2. **Single-author universe.** One hand, one voice, growing catalog. Buyers join a creator, not a single SKU.
3. **Bilingual native craft.** Copy is authored — not translated — in Slovak and English. That alone separates Walterin from 95% of European indie decks.
4. **Central-European literary atmosphere.** Not Anglo-American occult, not Scandinavian minimalism, not California wellness. A specific cultural register that reads on the page.
5. **Editorial product treatment.** The packaging, copy, and product pages are designed like an exhibition wall label, not an e-commerce listing.

- Hardcore esoteric / occult tarot communities — we won't optimize copy or imagery for them.

## Brand Personality & Voice

### Personality archetype: The Storyteller

Walterin is the friend who reads, watches films, walks cities, and tells you about them in a way that makes you wish you'd been there. Bold, warm, literary. Not academic. Not corporate. Slovak literary sensibility softened by humor; cinematic when the artwork demands, intimate when it doesn't.

### Personality traits

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| Bold | Confident lines, no hedging. "This is what it is." |
| Warm | Speaks to a reader, not a buyer. Uses "you" sparingly but generously. |
| Literary | Knows when to use a single-word sentence. Rhythm matters. |
| Specific | Names places, years, faces, fragments. Refuses the generic. |
| Restrained | Doesn't fill the room. Lets the artwork breathe. |

### Voice guidelines

**DO:**
- Lead with image and story. Set a scene, name a feeling, then stop.
- Use rhythm — short sentence, longer sentence, breath.
- Be specific: a city, a year, a face, a fragment of a myth.
- Treat copy as a companion to the artwork, not a label for it.
- Match the atmosphere of the card or page being shown.

**DON'T:**
- Generic spiritual language ("the universe is guiding you", "tap into your inner wisdom").
- Marketing clichés ("must-have", "elevate", "transform your life").
- Spoiler-heavy descriptions of cards or stories. Tease, don't summarize.
- Corporate hedging.
- Hashtag soup. Hashtags are signposts, not seasoning.

### Bilingual rule

Slovak and English are equal first languages. Copy must be authored natively in both — never translated word-for-word. When the same concept lands differently in each language, write the version that lands best in that language and let the other adapt around it. A native Slovak reader and a native English reader should both feel the brand was made for them.

## Recommended Marketing Channels

### Tier 1 — Activate at launch

**Instagram**
- Primary visual platform for the target demographic.
- Content mix: 50% artwork (single-card reveals, atmospheric crops), 30% behind-the-scenes (studio, drawing in progress, packaging), 20% story / universe (captions that tease the world without spoiling cards).
- Reels: drawing process, deck reveal, time-lapse.
- KPI: saves per post (intent), profile visits → link clicks, follower growth.

**Facebook**
- Primary platform for Walter's existing SK/CZ/DE readership.
- Long-form captions in SK + DE. Community building. Launch-day announcement.
- KPI: organic reach within Walter's existing reader audience, link clicks to product page.

**Email**
- The owned channel — start collecting addresses immediately, before launch.
- Sequence: pre-launch tease → launch announcement → first-card reveal → social-proof sequence → restock / next-product first-access.
- KPI: list size growth, open rate, launch-day click-through.

### Tier 2 — Activate within 1–3 months of launch

**Pinterest**
- Discovery-to-purchase funnel for atmospheric / illustration-led product categories.
- Organic: boards by theme (the universe, the studio, the deck).
- Pins: single cards on clean backgrounds + universe atmosphere boards driving to product page.
- KPI: outbound clicks to product page, not impressions.

**Press & blogs**
- SK / CZ literary and design press for the launch story (illustrator → object).
- Indie illustration / tarot blogs for English-language reach.
- KPI: domain referrals, audience-quality of incoming traffic.

### Tier 3 — Test with limited budget after launch traction

**TikTok**
- Best content type: process — drawing in progress, deck reveal, studio walkthroughs.
- Don't replicate Instagram. TikTok rewards unpolished, authentic, inside-the-studio content.
- KPI: video completion rate, follower growth, traffic spikes on launch days.

**Meta paid (Instagram + Facebook ads)**
- Retargeting first — visitors who viewed the product page or added to cart.
- Cold prospecting only after organic and warm audiences are saturated.
- KPI: ROAS on retargeting first; cold-prospect tests measured on landing-page intent (time on page, add-to-cart).

## Geographic Strategy

### Phase 1 — Establish (launch month → month 3): Slovakia + Czechia + DACH

- **Slovakia + Czechia:** native-language audience, Walter's strongest existing readership. Facebook + email + SK/CZ press do the heavy lifting.
- **DACH (Germany / Austria):** German edition of *Bratislava (Walterin)* already exists — there is a warm reader pool to activate. Captions and store experience in DE where realistic; otherwise EN with clear shipping.

### Phase 2 — Expand (months 3–6): English-speaking EU + UK + US

- **Netherlands, Ireland, broader EU:** English-comfortable audiences, low localization barrier.
- **UK:** test after Phase 1; Brexit shipping/duties must be solved cleanly in checkout before paid spend.
- **US:** test on Instagram organic reach first; only consider paid once an English-language conversion baseline exists.

### Phase 3 — Evaluate (month 6+)

- French / Italian / Spanish markets — language localization is a real cost; only commit when there's signal that those audiences are converting in English already.
- Specialty distribution (indie bookstores, design shops, museum gift shops) for select cities — only after the first print run sells through.

### What not to do geographically
- Don't spread the launch budget across every market simultaneously. Win the home + adjacent markets first.
- Don't enter the US with paid ads before an organic English-language baseline exists.
- Don't translate the website into a third language for the sake of it — quality of bilingual SK/EN matters more than breadth.

## Visual Identity at Launch

### Product page atmosphere
The product page for Comics Tarot of Consciousness should read like an exhibition wall label, not an e-commerce listing. Generous whitespace, the illustration as hero, copy as a literary companion. Avoid default Shopify section patterns — feature product, related products, trust badges — wherever they undercut the editorial tone.

### Tuck box anatomy (locked)
- Walterin logo at top of front face
- "COMICS TAROT" set large (Walterin Bold)
- "of Consciousness" subordinated below
- The Magician card on the front face
- Brand red panel on the back

This is a brand-defining piece of design. Do not redesign without a specific reason.

### Photography & social art direction
- The illustration is the hero. Don't compete with it.
- Lifestyle: low ambient light, warm tones, suggestion of a reader's environment (desk, book, coffee, window). Never staged crystal-and-incense tarot tropes.
- Card reveals: flat scans + selective close-ups that emphasize linework and atmosphere.

### Durability / craft framing in copy
Don't say "high quality" — every brand says this. Make craft concrete:
- Name the print run size when relevant ("first run of 150").
- Name the maker (single illustrator, named, with a body of prior work).
- Describe what the buyer holds — card stock, finish, tuck-box construction — in specific language, not adjectives.

## Open Questions Before Hard Launch

These should be locked in before paid ads or hard launch creative goes live.

1. **Retail price.** Single-unit pricing for Comics Tarot of Consciousness, plus any bundle (deck + signed print? deck + booklet?).
2. **Languages on the storefront at launch.** SK + EN both live day one, or SK first / EN within 30 days?
3. **Launch geography.** SK-only soft launch, or SK + DACH + EN simultaneously?
4. **Reviews tooling.** Which review widget — and is it live before or after the first 50 units ship?
5. **Pre-order vs. drop.** Pre-order while units finish printing, or hold until everything is in hand?
6. **Universe roadmap.** Do we tease the next product at launch, or hold it back to build anticipation?
7. **Press strategy.** Does Walter want named SK/CZ press involvement, or does Veronka run it solo?
