## 14-day right of withdrawal

You can withdraw from a purchase without giving a reason within 14 days. The period starts on the day you receive the goods (for several parcels from one order, the day you receive the last one). For an eBook, it starts on the day the contract is concluded.

**How to withdraw:**
- online via the **"Withdraw from contract here"** link in the footer; we send you a confirmation by email without undue delay;
- by email to support@walterin.com;
- by post to Walterin s. r. o., Ľubochnianska 4, 831 04 Bratislava, Slovakia.

You may use the model withdrawal form, but you don't have to. An unequivocal statement sent before the period ends is enough.

**Returning the goods:** send the goods within 14 days of withdrawing to [TO CONFIRM – return address; for now Walterin s. r. o., Ľubochnianska 4, 831 04 Bratislava, Slovakia]. You bear the direct cost of returning the goods. You are only liable for any diminished value resulting from handling other than what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.

**Refund:** within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal we refund all payments, including delivery (up to the cost of the least expensive standard delivery we offer), using your original payment method, free of charge. We may wait until the goods are back or you show that you sent them, whichever is earlier.

**eBooks:** if before downloading you expressly agreed that delivery may start before the withdrawal period ends and confirmed that you thereby lose the right of withdrawal, and we confirmed this consent to you by email, you can no longer withdraw from the eBook purchase.

## Claims for defects

We are liable for defects in goods for 24 months from delivery, and for defects in eBooks for 24 months from supply. If a defect in goods appears within this period, it is presumed to have existed at delivery.

**How to claim:** write to support@walterin.com with your order number, a description of the defect and what you ask for (repair or replacement; if neither is possible, a price reduction or a refund). Tell us about the defect within two months of discovering it. We confirm your claim by email and settle it within 30 days at the latest. We pay the cost of sending the claimed goods.

**Damaged or wrong item:** write to us, with a photo if you can. We replace damaged or wrong goods; if that is not possible, we refund you. We bear all costs.

## Disputes

If you are not satisfied, ask us for redress at support@walterin.com. If we reject your request or do not reply within 30 days, you can turn to an alternative dispute resolution body, in particular the Slovak Trade Inspection (ars@soi.sk), or another body on the list of the Slovak Ministry of Economy.
