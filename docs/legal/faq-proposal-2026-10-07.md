## Category 5 · Returns, refunds and guarantee · *Vrátenie, peniaze a záruka*

Yes. You have 14 days from the day the parcel arrives, and you don't have to give a reason. Use the

back within 14 days of telling us. You pay the return postage. We refund everything you paid,
including the cheapest standard delivery we offer, within 14 days.

**What about eBooks?**
Different, and you agree to it before you pay: once the download starts, the right to withdraw is
gone. That's the tick-box in the cart, and it's there because you get the file immediately.

**What if a book turns up faulty?** ✓ *(one sentence added — see note below)*
By law we are responsible for faults for two years from the day the item reaches you. If a book
arrives damaged, misprinted or with pages missing, write to support@walterin.com and we will repair
or replace it. If neither of those works, you get your money back. **The same two years apply to an
eBook, counted from the day we send you the file.** This is separate from changing your mind, which
is the 14 days above. The official EU notice about these rights, which we are not allowed to alter,
is in our **Terms, clause 10**, and behind **Legal guarantee** at the bottom of every page.

**"Two years" is the Terms' "24 months".** Clause 9.1 counts in months; the official EU notice
counts in years ("Minimum two-year legal guarantee protection"), and so does a person. Same period,
and the FAQ follows the notice rather than the contract because it is the thing being explained.

**The guarantee covers eBooks, and the first draft of this missed it.** Clause 9.1 says *"and for
defects in eBooks within 24 months of supply"* — caught by cross-reading the Terms rather than by
re-reading my own answer. The sentence is marked in Returns above.

**Every number is sourced.** 180 pages and 100 decks per language from what is already published;
two years' liability from § 5(1)(f) and the Terms; 14 days from Terms 8.1; the payment list from
Terms 5.1 with Apple Pay and Google Pay confirmed against the Shopify API — no PayPal, because the
Terms mark it as not active.

**The address answer earns its place.** Clause 8.5 still says the return address is to be confirmed,
so the FAQ says where we are and tells people to write first, rather than inviting parcels to an
office that may not be the return address.

**Slovak is written, not translated.** *"Niet čo doručovať ani čo platiť"*, *"Dobierku neponúkame"*,
*"S rozmyslením si to nesúvisí"* — none of these are the English sentence with Slovak words in it.
