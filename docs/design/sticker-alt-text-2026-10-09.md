## Three notes before you approve

**1 · Image 5 shows an Apple logo and image 2 and 5 show a shield that reads as Ferrari's.** The alt
text says "the maker's logo" and "a shield with a black prancing horse" rather than naming either
brand — alt text is published copy, and naming someone else's mark in it is a claim we do not need
to make. The drawings themselves are Walter's call, not mine.

**2 · Nothing here says waterproof, dishwasher-safe or weatherproof**, and nothing should until we
know. The sheets are described only as matte.

**3 · This is a store write when you approve it.** Alt text lives on the product's media, not in
the theme, so it goes: backup → old vs new → your OK → write → read back.
