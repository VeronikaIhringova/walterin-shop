4 · The walk, every frame the same length

Unified, and here is how. Setting one duration by the longest frame would have put every frame at 11s and the run at 82s — a fifth longer than before. So the row explanations were shortened instead, from 16–21 words to 8–10, which made the frame that opens a row fit the same beat as the two that follow. They read better short: "What is going on, and what to notice in it." · "What is yours, and what to admit before anything moves." · "Where it leads, and what you can change."

Every frame is held 7s in English, 8s in Slovak, plus a 0.8s beat where the row turns — 64.8s and 73.8s for the whole run, measured on the build, the same length as the variable version it replaces. 7s is the top of NN/g's 5–7s floor, so even the lightest frames get a comfortable beat. The dot for the frame you are on fills with yellow from the bottom as its time runs out; frames behind you are solid ink, frames ahead are empty. It never starts on its own and one press or one click anywhere takes it back. Numbers and sources in docs/design/AUTOPLAY-WALK.md.

6 · The arrow

One line of even weight with a small solid head. It leaves the note beside the row it points at and follows a single gentle curve; the last part of the curve lies on the straight line running into the centre of that frame, so the head points exactly at it. It stops 10px short of the card at every frame and both widths — measured on all nine, in both languages. No wobble, no improvised shapes: the only thing that changes between frames is where it is aimed.
