The three arrows, at the size they will be

Drawn, not stroked: each one is a filled marker shape with an uneven edge, a weight that grows along the stroke, and a solid head. The three are separate drawings — the climb accelerates upward and thickens as it goes, the level one has the slight double waver of a hand holding a line, the fall leaves the note flat, dips, and lands with a heavier head. The dashed rules are the note's edge and the card's edge: the point stops at the card and never enters the artwork.
