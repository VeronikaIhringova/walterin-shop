Language switcher — two options for phones

The bar is 390px wide and already holds a menu button, the logo and two icons. Both options keep the logo centred, never wrap, and give the control a 44×44 tap target.

Option A · It lives in the menu

Nothing new in the bar at all. The two languages sit at the bottom of the hamburger menu as full-width buttons, where there is room to name them properly. This is what the theme was always built to do — it just never worked.
