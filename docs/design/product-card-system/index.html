Product card system — three variations

One calm card. A category top-left, at most one status pill top-right, and a wide flat bar along the bottom of the image that carries the date. The three variations differ only in how the labels are filled and shaped — the structure, the copy and the rules are identical. Pick a variation to open the interactive version: hover the cards, tab through them, switch Digital ↔ Printed and toggle 1440 / 390. Full rules: docs/design/PRODUCT-CARD-SYSTEM.md.
