Sold out, and the notify-me

A sold-out card says Sold out plainly. On hover — and always on a phone — the bar offers the thing the visitor actually wants: “Tell me when it is back” / “Dajte mi vedieť”, which opens the same notify form the product page already uses. Saying only "Sold out" tells someone the door is shut; this hands them the bell.

Recommendation

B — Paper. It is the closest to what you liked about rhode: the artwork stays the loudest thing on the card and the bar is quiet until it matters. A is the design system's button shrunk onto a photograph, and four of them in a row shout over the work. C is the prettiest and the least safe — backdrop-filter is the exact class of thing that has behaved differently in Safari twice already, and a bar that turns unreadable over a pale image is worse than a plain one.

If you want more presence than B, the smallest step is B with a yellow fill on hover only, which is already how it behaves here.
