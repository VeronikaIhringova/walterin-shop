# Site-wide UI audit — old style still on the site

Read-only, 30 Sep 2026. Nothing changed. Evidence is `file:line`. "Renders" means reachable from a
live `order` array or live code path, cross-checked against the templates, section groups and
`config/settings_data.json`.

## The shape of it

**On the design system:** 9 sections (`walterin-buy`, `walterin-faq`, `walterin-pages`,
`walterin-where`, `walterin-withdrawal`, `meet-the-cards`, `how-a-card-is-read`, `meet-walter`
partly, `header` language switcher) and 3 snippets (`wui-button`, `wui-icon`, `legal-guarantee`).

**Still stock Dawn/Shrine and live:** header, announcement bar, footer, cart drawer (on every page),
predictive search, the whole home page, collection, cart, search, 404, blog, article,
list-collections, contact form, and all seven customer-account templates.

Two structural notes: `walterin-ui.css` still has **no card component and no page-header
primitive** — wave 0 of `DESIGN-SYSTEM-ROLLOUT.md` was never finished. And **Cormorant Garamond
appears nowhere in the theme**, only in mock-ups, despite being named in the ground truth as the
literary companion face.

**Dead code — not live problems.** Roughly 20 `"disabled": true` sections, 10 sections referenced
nowhere, 10 snippets reachable only from dead sections, and **29 of 32 `ai_gen_block_*` files**
unused. Listed at the end so nobody fixes something invisible.

## Fix in the theme

### High

**F1 · The announcement bar is a grey band, wrong ink, wrong radius — on every page.**
`sections/header-group.json:26` uses `background-2` (which is `#f3f3f3`, see F23); `:35` forces
`color: #121212` (not ink `#222222`); `:36` sets `border-radius: 12px` and raw 10px margins.

**F2 · The home page paints the header white and yanks the page up.**
`layout/theme.liquid:430–524`: `color: white !important` on menu links, announcement text and
icons; `main#MainContent { margin-top: -70px }`; and hover `#fed501` — a **fourth yellow**, not
`#FCF205`. `sections/header.liquid:110–113` repeats it for the language switcher.
Also, the `.utility-bar…` rules at `:440` and `:477` are **dead selectors** — no such element
exists in this theme.

**F3 · The home page swaps the logo for a white raster PNG** and forces the cart icon white
(`templates/index.json:6`). The same block defines a **second type scale** that nothing uses.

**F4 · Grey product-card background on the home page** — `#f3f3f3 !important` on `.card--card`.

**F5 · A 35px blurred shadow on every product, collection and blog card.**
`assets/component-card.css:66` resolves to `1rem 1rem 3.5rem rgba(34,34,34,.05)` with the live
settings. Renders on collections, home, cart carousel, search, related products.
⚠️ Fixed in **admin** (F24), not code — and note the standing decision that the shop-grid cards stay
as they are. The shadow setting is a smaller question than redesigning the card.

**F6 · Grey newsletter band** on 404, article, list-collections and cart (`background-2`).

**F7 · `meet-walter` is off-palette on the tarot page.** `sections/meet-walter.liquid:50`
`box-shadow: 0 24px 56px rgba(0,0,0,0.18)` — a real soft shadow, **not overridden**; `:48`
`border-radius: 14px`; `:58`/`:67` `color: #2e2a39` (purple-tinted grey, not ink); `:32`/`:170`
`#FFFEF7` — **one digit off paper** `#FFFDF7`; `:63` 15px, off the type scale.

## Change in Shopify admin settings

These live in `config/settings_data.json` → Theme settings. Fixes here are settings, not code.

### High

**F23 · `colors_background_2` is `#f3f3f3` — grey.** This one value is the source of the
announcement bar band (F1) and every grey newsletter band (F6). *Fix: `#FFFDF7`.*

**F24 · Card shadows are soft** — blur 35, offsets 10/10, opacity 5, on product, collection and blog
cards. This is what F5 renders. *Fix: opacity 0, or blur 0 with 4/4 offsets and full opacity for the
hard ink shadow.*

**F25 · Yellow is `#fce206`, not `#FCF205`** — on both accent slots. `accent-2` is the sale-badge
scheme, so the badge renders in the wrong yellow.

### Medium

**F26 · Every radius is off the 8px token** — buttons 40 (pill), variant pills 40, inputs 6, media
12, cards 4, collection cards 12, blog cards 12, text boxes 24, popups 22, badges 6.

**F27 · Section padding is per-section raw pixels.** `spacing_sections: 0`, so each section supplies
its own: newsletter 68/52, featured-collection 44/44 and 76/36, search 36/36, collection grid 24/44,
blog 0/64, contact 48/92, rich-text 40/28… The home, collection, cart, search, blog, 404,
list-collections and customer pages therefore have **no shared rhythm at all**. Product and page
templates are already zeroed by the design system; the rest are not.

**F28 · `colors_outline_button_labels: rgba(0,0,0,0)` becomes pure black.** Liquid reads only the
RGB channels, so the alpha is discarded and every Dawn link draws at `rgba(0,0,0,.85)` — not ink.

**F29 · `type_header_font` is Inter 500.** Harmless for h1–h5 (forced to WalterinBold), but it is
what h6 and some Shrine headings fall back to.

### Low

**F30** Legacy `checkout_accent_color: #3bc2c3` (teal) — ignored by current Shopify checkout, which
is branded in Settings → Checkout. **F31** Soft popup shadow, but the popup is disabled.
**F32** The Shrine demo gradients survive under `presets` — they return if anyone hits "reset".
**F33** Payment icons are already correct: no PayPal, no Revolut. The warning in the rollout doc is
out of date.

## Policy pages — an important correction to my own plan

Confirmed: the body class is `t-policy`, `walterin-ui.css` **is** loaded, and
`grep -rn "t-policy"` across the theme returns **nothing** — no rule ever matches a policy page. The
only policy rule in the theme is `assets/base.css:664` `padding-bottom: 3rem`. There is **no
`max-width` and no horizontal padding anywhere**, so the legal text runs to whatever width `<main>`
gives it.

**But adding `.t-policy` to the seven gated rules would, on its own, change nothing.** Six of the
seven match `.shopify-section` descendants — and a policy page has **no sections at all**. The
seventh matches `.page-width`, `.wui-wrap`, `.mw-inner`, `.fwi-inner`, none of which exist in
Shopify's policy markup.

So `docs/design/POLICY-PAGE-TOC.md` step 1 as I wrote it is wrong. The real work is:
- add `.shopify-policy__container` to the frame selector at `walterin-ui.css:159`, **and**
- add new rules styling `.shopify-policy__title h1` as `wui-title` and `.shopify-policy__body .rte`
  as `wui-rte`.

I have corrected the plan document.
