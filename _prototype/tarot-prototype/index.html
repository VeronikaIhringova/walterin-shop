The illustrator

Why a comic artist redrew the tarot

Draft: Walter rewrites in his own words

“A tarot card is already a comic. One picture with a whole story folded inside. I unfolded it.”

For years I drew cities like Bratislava and Prague as illustrated stories: history told with a wink. The tarot pulled me the same way. Seventy-eight old pictures that everyone recognises and few people really read.

So I kept the Rider–Waite skeleton and drew each card again as nine frames, the way I draw everything: by hand, in ink, one line after another. The cards don't tell you what will happen. They show you a small story and let you find yourself in it.

Walter Ihring
